Introduction to National Agencies

The Federal Republic of Botswenia has various national agencies and institutions responsible for specialised administrative, regulatory, security and public service functions.

These agencies operate within the national administrative framework and work under their respective ministries or designated government authorities.

Central Security and Strategic Affairs Bureau (CSSAB)

The Central Security and Strategic Affairs Bureau (CSSAB) is a national institution of the Federal Republic of Botswenia. It operates within the country's national security and strategic affairs framework.

CSSAB is responsible for matters related to national security, strategic coordination and security-related institutional functions.

Central National Police Force (CNPF)

The Central National Police Force (CNPF) is a national security and law enforcement institution of the Federal Republic of Botswenia.

It forms part of the country's national policing framework and contributes to maintaining law and order and supporting public security.

Overseas Criminal Affairs Investigation Bureau (OCAIB)

The Overseas Criminal Affairs Investigation Bureau (OCAIB) is a national agency of the Federal Republic of Botswenia responsible for matters concerning overseas criminal affairs and international investigative coordination.

The bureau deals with criminal affairs involving overseas jurisdictions and supports cooperation on relevant international investigations.

Botswenia Space Exploration and Research Organisation (BSERO)

The Botswenia Space Exploration and Research Organisation (BSERO) is a national space research institution of the Federal Republic of Botswenia.

It is associated with space exploration, scientific research and the development of space-related technologies.

🛡️ Consumer Safety and Product Standards Authority (CSPSA)

The Consumer Safety and Product Standards Authority is responsible for protecting consumers and ensuring that products meet established safety and quality standards across Botswenia.

Conclusion

National agencies play an essential role in maintaining the administration, security, public safety and institutional development of the Federal Republic of Botswenia. Each agency operates with specialised responsibilities and contributes to the effective functioning of the country's national systems.

From strategic security and law enforcement to overseas criminal investigations, space research and consumer protection, these institutions help safeguard national interests and maintain public confidence. Their specialised functions support cooperation, accountability, regulatory standards and the overall progress of the nation.

The agencies described in this section represent only part of Botswenia's wider institutional framework. Together, they form an important foundation for national governance, public service and long-term development.
