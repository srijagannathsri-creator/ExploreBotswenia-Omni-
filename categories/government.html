Government of Botswenia

The Federal Republic of Botswenia follows a Parliamentary Republic system of government. Its political and administrative framework is based on the Constitution, which establishes the institutions, powers and responsibilities of the government.

The government consists of the executive and legislative institutions, with the President serving as the Head of State and the Prime Minister leading the government.

Executive Branch

The Executive Branch of the Federal Republic of Botswenia is responsible for implementing laws, managing government administration and directing national policies.

The executive leadership consists of the President and the Prime Minister. The President serves as the Head of State, while the Prime Minister leads the government and oversees its day-to-day administration.

President of Botswenia

The President of the Federal Republic of Botswenia serves as the Head of State and Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces. The President plays an important role in national leadership, defence and foreign affairs.

The President is elected by Parliament for a term of seven years and performs constitutional duties in accordance with the Constitution of Botswenia.

Prime Minister of Botswenia

The Prime Minister is the Head of Government of the Federal Republic of Botswenia and leads the country's executive administration.

The Prime Minister serves a four-year term and is selected through an internal vote by the majority party in the House of Representatives. The Prime Minister is responsible for leading government policies, coordinating ministries and overseeing national administration.

Parliament of Botswenia

The Parliament of the Federal Republic of Botswenia is the country's national legislative institution. It is responsible for lawmaking, parliamentary oversight and representing the interests of the people and states.

The Parliament consists of two chambers: the House of Representatives and the House of States. Together, they form the bicameral legislative structure of Botswenia.

House of Representatives (HR)

The House of Representatives is the lower chamber of the Parliament of the Federal Republic of Botswenia. It consists of 650 members elected through the First-Past-the-Post (FPTP) electoral system.

A political party or coalition must secure at least 326 seats to obtain a parliamentary majority. The House plays a central role in legislation, government formation and parliamentary accountability.

House of States (HS)

The House of States is the upper chamber of the Parliament of the Federal Republic of Botswenia. It consists of 250 members and represents the states within the national legislative framework.

Members are nominated by the President and their appointments require parliamentary approval. The House participates in the legislative process and provides representation of state interests at the national level.

Elections in Botswenia

Elections in the Federal Republic of Botswenia form an essential part of its parliamentary democratic system. Members of the House of Representatives are elected through the First-Past-the-Post (FPTP) electoral system.

The Prime Minister is selected through an internal vote by the majority party in the House of Representatives. The President is elected by Parliament for a seven-year term.

Conclusion

The government of the Federal Republic of Botswenia operates through a parliamentary framework established by its Constitution. Its executive and legislative institutions work together to maintain national administration, democratic representation and constitutional governance.
